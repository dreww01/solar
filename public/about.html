Our Story

Founded over a decade ago, SolarTech Nigeria began with a simple vision: to provide reliable, affordable solar energy solutions to homes and businesses across Nigeria. What started as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into one of the nation's most trusted solar installation companies.

We've witnessed firsthand the transformative power of solar energy - from small businesses that can now operate 24/7, to families who no longer worry about power outages. Every installation we complete is a step toward a more sustainable, energy-independent Nigeria.

Today, with over 500 successful installations across all 36 states, we continue to innovate and expand our services, always keeping our customers' needs at the heart of everything we do.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class solar energy solutions that empower Nigerian homes and businesses with reliable, sustainable power. We're committed to excellence in every installation, exceptional customer service, and making renewable energy accessible to all.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria's leading renewable energy company, recognized for innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction. We envision a future where every Nigerian has access to clean, affordable solar power, contributing to a sustainable and prosperous nation.
