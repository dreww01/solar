Frequently Asked Questions

How long does installation take?

Most residential installations are completed within 1-3 days, depending on system size. Commercial projects may take longer.

Do you offer financing options?

Yes, we work with several financial partners to offer flexible payment plans that make solar energy accessible to everyone.

What warranty do you provide?

We offer comprehensive warranties: 25 years on solar panels, 10 years on inverters, and 2 years on installation workmanship.

How much can I save with solar?

Savings vary based on your energy consumption and system size, but most customers see 50-80% reduction in electricity costs.
